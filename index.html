Your speed, straight from GPS.

Dash is a GPS speedometer for car, bike, cycling and walking. Big digits or an analog gauge, a mirrored HUD for your windshield, a speed limit alert and trips that keep recording with the screen off.

Everything a speedometer should do

No maps to download, no account, no connection needed.

Digits or gauge

A huge digital readout, or an analog dial with a needle. Switch between km/h, mph and knots at any time.

Speed limit alert

Set your limit. Dash turns the speed red and beeps and vibrates when you go over, without flickering around the limit.

Trips with the screen off

Start, pause and stop. A foreground service keeps recording and shows your live speed in the notification.

Route, speed and altitude

Every trip shows its route colored by speed, a speed-over-time chart and an altitude profile with total climb, drawn on your phone with no map tiles.

G-force meter

Live acceleration, braking and cornering from the accelerometer. Hard braking and sharp corners are marked on the trip's route.

Automatic trips

With Dash open, a trip starts when you drive off and ends after 5 minutes parked, even with the screen off.

Home-screen widget

Your odometer and last trip at a glance, with a button to start or stop a trip.

Open in Google Maps

Open any trip in Google Maps or share it as a link. No account and no API key.

Odometer and GPX

A running total of every trip, and one tap to export any trip as a GPX file to share or import elsewhere.

Trustworthy numbers

Poor GPS fixes and jumps are thrown out, speed is smoothed, and standing still really reads zero.

HUD mode for the windshield

Lay your phone flat on the dashboard and the speed reflects in the glass.

  1. Tap the speedometer icon in the top bar of the home screen.
  2. Dash goes landscape with pure black, giant mirrored digits and full screen brightness.
  3. Place the phone face up under the windshield. The digits read the right way round in the reflection. Use the flip button if you want them unmirrored.
Set the phone up before you drive and never look at it for long. Dash is an aid, not a certified speedometer: your car's own speedometer and the road signs are what count.

Permissions and privacy

Your trips stay on your phone. The only network call is the update check in Settings.

LocationPrecise location, to read your speed and position from GPS. Used only while Dash is open or recording a trip.
Foreground serviceKeeps the GPS running while a trip is recording and the screen is off.
NotificationsShows your live speed during a trip (Android 13 and newer).
VibrateFor the speed limit alert. You can switch it off.
InternetOnly for the update check in Settings. Google Maps opens only when you tap it.

Requirements

Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer, and a phone with GPS. GPS works best with a clear view of the sky.

Install

Dash is not on Google Play. Tap Download APK on your phone and open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. Or install Ash Store once and get updates from it.
